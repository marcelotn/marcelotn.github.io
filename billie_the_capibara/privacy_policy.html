Privacy Policy

Last updated: 08/10/2025

This app was developed exclusively to perform unit conversion calculations. We value user privacy, and this policy clarifies that no personal information is collected, stored, or shared.

1. Information Collection

This app does not collect any personal data from users. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Geographic location
  • Device information or usage behavior

All conversion inputs are processed locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to external servers.

2. System Permissions

The app requests permission to access local storage on the device. This permission is used solely to save the last status of unit conversions, allowing users to resume their activity seamlessly. These saved values are not shared or accessed by third parties.

3. Use of Information

Since no personal data is collected, no information is used for marketing, analytics, or advertising purposes.

4. Data Sharing

This app does not share any data with third parties, as no personal or sensitive information is collected or transmitted.

5. Security

The app is built following best security practices to ensure that locally stored data remains protected and accessible only by the app itself.

6. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Users are encouraged to review this section periodically. Any significant changes will be communicated through the app interface.

7. Contact
